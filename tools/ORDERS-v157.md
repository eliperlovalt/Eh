## The fifteen
1. **The Observatory.** Four globes, one in each corner: a true voxel sphere with blue sea and green and tan land, an upright gold meridian ring round the ball (not a flat tray under it), a turned wooden stand on three feet; each turned differently. Remove the brown stick on the window sill. The holo globe opens the newest Globe page, not the Data Hub, through the private device map only; if the map's `globe` key points at the Data Hub, report what it points at (KAIROS gets the link from GOLEM).
2. **The Stalls.** Six proper stalls: low wooden partitions about 1.5 blocks high, a post at each end, straight edges, a hay rack and a bucket in each; no jagged stepped slab. Horses shaped as horses (body, legs, neck, head, mane, tail) in bay, chestnut and grey, or leave the stalls empty if that cannot read. Remove the floating brown-and-gold beam on the west wall. Glaze the windows; no outline-only frames. Iron stable lanterns on wall hooks in place of the chandeliers. A dark beamed timber ceiling in place of the white slat grid.
3. **The stair down to Pardes** (the stairwell in the Tower Porch). Remove the green slab lying over it. Widen to 3 blocks, stone treads, a rail on the open side, a lamp at the bottom. A stone surround on the floor opening. Prove a walk down into Pardes.
4. **Beside the path to the Chapel, west of the Hall.** A grey box with red and blue slabs, a red plank cut through the wall, a white slab floating. Find what it is in the plan and build it in the house's stone: a coped garden wall with a gateway for the chapel path, or a small loggia on columns.
5. **The Long Gallery, at the rail over the Great Hall.** Delete the purple and white rod (three sweeps). Trim the gallery floor slab that slices across the painted ceiling. Remove the loose candle stub on the rail.
6. **The Long Gallery.** The huge blank cream wall standing up through the floor round the Great Hall void: open it as a balustraded void, or panel it like the other gallery walls (pilasters, cornice, pictures).
7. **The Grand Stair.** The red runner on every tread and riser, following the steps, centred, with an even stone margin. Gold rods the runner's width, one a step, at the back of the tread.
8. **The Women's Stair.** Fix the floating lantern to the wall on a bracket. Rebuild the half-sunk orange landing rail in the stair's white-painted style, standing on the floor with a clean bottom rail.
9. **The Grand Stair Landing (red walls).** Delete the lone white pole. End the red wall cleanly at the dado; no red over the gold rail. Remove the loose gold bracket. Run the gold rails to newel posts at both ends.
10. **The Great Hall.** A long refectory table down the centre with the ladder-back chairs in facing pairs along it; no loose chairs. Move the sconce off the chair onto the wall. Keep the space before the paintings clear.
11. **The Oak Stair.** A full oak balustrade (rail, balusters, newels) up the open side. Delete the floating rail piece at the top. A straight stringer where the stair meets the wall; no jagged step edge.
12. and 13. **The Great Hall dais door behind the thrones.** Full height, a carved architrave and a pediment, no blank wall above. Two mirror-image leaves, panels aligned, a dark meeting stile, no bright gap. Kairos's seated arms rest on the throne arms without passing into them. The napkin a thin draped cloth (done in v1.56).
14. **The Music Room.** The two portraits become two different sitters (dress, hair, backgrounds), each with eyes and a mouth; both hung fully on the wall. The cornice unbroken over the doorway, an architrave round the door.
15. **The Park, the front steps of the Hall.** Break the flight into three runs with two wide landings. A stone balustrade with urns on both sides. Stone texture, a darker nosing on each step.
